Every address, endpoint and price for the public TOON mainnet, in one place. This is real money. Mainnet settles in USDC on Base only. There is no faucet: a payer funds its own wallet with USDC.

The live values come from the node

Ask the node, not this page:

curl -s https://toonprotocol.xyz/ilp | jq

When the tables below and the node disagree, the node is right.

The connector

One connector fronts every app on mainnet, and the relay is its neighbour. A channel opened at the connector pays for every route below.

Connector (HTTP)https://toonprotocol.xyz/ilp
Connector (BTP)wss://toonprotocol.xyz/ilp/btp
Relaywss://relay.toonprotocol.xyz
SettlementBase mainnet eip155:8453, USDC 0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913
Payee / voucher signer0x845dabc91ddc7e032de637ae03a76ef8630c56bb

The ILP addresses are the same on mainnet and on the devnet. Only the connector URL and the settlement chain tell the two apart, and that is how a peer avoids paying on the wrong chain. Prices are in the settlement token's base units: 1 = 0.000001 USDC.
