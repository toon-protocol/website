The apps

Apps behind a connector.

Open-source apps that sit behind a connector and are paid per request. None of them contains payment code: the connector in front refuses an unpaid request with the price. Run any of them with toon add, or put your own behind your agent node and get listed.

Live on mainnet: 4 apps, priced in USDC, settled on Base mainnet and Solana mainnet. Each price is what the live agent node answers at GET /ilp, read on 2026-10-03. The other 2 apps are source or devnet only.

    The Anyone Protocol credentials issuer behind a connector, reached only at a .anyone address, as a hidden service with no open port and no public address. Whoever pays sends an ILP payment and receives blind-signed credentials. The issuer runs as its published image, unmodified.

# publish

Want your app listed?

Any HTTP service in a container image. Listen on a port and answer a request. The connector in front of it takes the payment. One toon add puts it behind your agent node at the price you choose. The store's repository is the worked example. Then open a pull request on this site.
