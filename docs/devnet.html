Reference

Devnet reference

The devnet connector, relay, routes and prices, and the faucet that funds you.

Every address, endpoint and price for the public TOON devnet, in one place. These are testnets. Base Sepolia and Solana devnet carry no real value, and the USDC on both is a mock mint anyone can draw from the faucet.

The live values come from the node

Ask the node, not this page:

curl -s https://toonprotocol.dev/ilp | jq

GET /ilp is free and unauthenticated. Its batchSettlements are the x402 channel terms the node is paid under, one per chain, and voucherSigners the keys it expects vouchers from. The tables below are a convenience. When the two disagree, the node is right, and the client never consults a preset in preference to the document a node answers with.

The connector

One connector fronts every app on the devnet, and the relay is its neighbour. A channel opened at the connector pays for every route below.

The ILP addresses are the same on the devnet and on mainnet. Only the connector URL and the settlement chain tell the two apart, and that is how a peer avoids paying on the wrong chain. Prices are in the settlement token's base units: 1 = 0.000001 USDC.

The faucet

faucet.devnet.toonprotocol.dev mints devnet USDC on both chains to any address. npx toon-client faucet calls it for the wallet in your keystore. The devnet USDC on Base Sepolia is minter-gated, so it comes from the faucet or nowhere.

The Echo Merchant

The Echo Merchant is a priced route at g.toon.echo, served by the connector at https://toonprotocol.dev, that sells nothing, on purpose. It costs 1 base unit plus 1 per KiB, on Base Sepolia or Solana devnet, and it answers each paid packet with a Mirror: the request as it arrived and the payment as it arrived, including what it was charged. Pay it first, before a route that sells something real, and a failure is your payer's, not the service's.

The walkthrough, the Echo flow, lives in the echo-merchant repository: install, fund, open a channel, pay, read the Mirror and withdraw.

Mainnet

Mainnet has its own connector, relay and payee, and settles on Base only. See the Mainnet reference.
